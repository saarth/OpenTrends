Trend intelligence

About Open Trends

A local browser app that turns Google Keyword Planner exports into theme-level trend analysis for any category. You supply the category searches and the keyword buckets; Open Trends reports what is moving.

App FAQ

What it does

Open Trends takes a Google Keyword Planner export (keywords plus monthly search volumes) and a dictionary of keyword buckets you define (themes), tags each search term against those buckets, and produces:

  • Theme-level size, share, latest month, short- and long-term growth
  • Velocity map (short-term vs long-term growth, sized by search volume)
  • Term-level drivers inside each theme
  • Brand and product-type attribution
  • Seasonality and monthly volume charts
  • Top searches per theme
  • A full Excel workbook and CSV/JSON exports

Everything runs in your browser. Your data is never uploaded anywhere.

Free and open source. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. A self-hostable version is available on GitHub.

Inputs

  • Category searches - a Google Keyword Planner export (.xlsx, .xls or .csv) with a keyword column, an AMS / average monthly searches column, and monthly date columns.
  • Keyword buckets (themes) - a dictionary mapping each theme name to a Boolean query or a plain list of terms. Upload under Category definitions or paste into the panel and press Apply dictionary.
  • Brands & products (optional) - brand and product-type dictionaries for a second attribution dimension. Upload them separately or as a combined file under Brand & product dictionary.

Outputs

  • Overview - KPIs, velocity map, fastest-growing themes, coverage breakdown
  • Theme growth - per-theme size, share, growth, monthly chart
  • Theme deep dive - term contributions, risers/drags, brand & product mix
  • Terms - term-level performance and keywords behind each term
  • Brands & products - brand/product tables, spotlight, filters
  • Top searches, Seasonality, Export

How to use it

  1. Open the app. A pre-computed sneakers & footwear example is already loaded as a demo.
  2. Drop in a Keyword Planner export (.xlsx / .xls / .csv). Check the detected sheet, short/long windows and dedupe setting.
  3. (Optional) Load or upload your own theme dictionary under Category definitions, and brand/product dictionaries under Brand & product dictionary (use the Load example … buttons to start from the bundled sneakers set).
  4. (Optional) Adjust the context toggles: treat product words as search context, or also match bare descriptor searches.
  5. Click Run analysis.
  6. Explore the tabs: Overview, Theme growth, Theme deep dive, Terms, Brands & products, Top searches, Seasonality.
  7. Use Export → Download full Excel workbook (or individual CSVs/JSON) for BI tools.
  8. To analyse your own category, upload or paste your own theme dictionary under Category definitions, then re-run.

Build themes, brands & product types with an LLM

You can use any LLM (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, …) to draft your dictionaries, then paste or upload them into the app:

  1. Export a sample of your Keyword Planner keywords (or describe the category) and paste it into the LLM.
  2. Ask for theme names with Boolean queries (OR, AND, NOT, NEAR/n, phrases, wildcards) or plain term lists, plus a brand alias list and a product-type list.
  3. Request the result as JSON in the app's dictionary shape (an object keyed by name; value is a query string or an array of terms), or as CSV/XLSX with Theme / Query columns.
  4. Paste into Category definitions / the brand panels, or save and upload the file, then Apply dictionary and re-run.

Tip: share a few example keywords and the desired number of themes; ask the LLM to keep product words (shoes, boots, sandals, …) as context so searches like "running shoe" still match.
